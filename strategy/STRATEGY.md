## 1. The situation in plain words

**Reach has fallen a lot.** A typical video got 2,793 views in 2023 and 608 in 2026 (01). In the last 12 months,
53% of posts got zero comments (01). Two things happened at once, and the data can't say which is bigger (09):
- **The platform:** accounts with 5,000 to 10,000 followers lost about 40% of their views per post in 2025 (05, 08).
  Unnie Mart's reach as a share of followers is in the same band as active Philippine peers (03).
- **The content and the volume:** 46 of the last 74 posts were sales posts, which perform exactly at the account's normal.
  The shop posted 7 times in the last 90 days, while active peers posted 26 to 50 times (01, 03).

**The hits have two things in common: timing and a tight format.** This is the most important finding (09, 11).

| Video | Views | What was coming |
|---|---|---|
| ENHYPEN lightstick "connect to the app" tutorial, Jan 25, 2025 | 225,400 | ENHYPEN's Bulacan concert, Mar 1, 2025 |
| SEVENTEEN Caratbong tutorial, Jan 14, 2025 | 30,500 | SEVENTEEN in Bulacan, Jan 18 to 19, 2025 |
| BLACKPINK lightstick tutorial, Mar 2, 2023 | 30,000 | BLACKPINK in Manila, late March 2023 (date from fan comments) |
| SEVENTEEN stock wall (6 seconds), Oct 5, 2023 | 71,600 | SEVENTEEN tour ticket window; 45 "I'll buy after I get a ticket" comments |
| BLACKPINK lightstick connect video, Jun 2025 | 913 | 82 seconds with a long intro; Philippine show 5 months away (BLACKPINK sales posts that week did well) |
| ENHYPEN lightstick post, May 2026 | 571 | Didn't show the steps ("the guide is out on YouTube, watch it"); no Philippine stop on the tour |

Both recent tutorial hits were short, numbered step-by-step videos (38 to 44 seconds) posted 4 days to 5 weeks
before a Philippine concert of that group. Both recent flops broke that format, and one also had no concert coming.
The good sales posts of 2022 to 2023 also sat inside fans' buying windows (01). With this few videos we can't
separate timing from format, **so the plan does both: the short step format, timed to shows and launches.**

**The audience tells us what they need** (01, 04). Note that 889 of the 1,015 fan comments come from 2022 to 2023,
because recent posts get few comments.
- **Money is the strongest emotion.** The most-liked comment in our sample of 1,057 comments is "next year na lang please 😭"
  (1,291 likes): fans begging a tour to wait until they've saved. Installment posts get 8 times more comments per
  view than on-hand and sale posts (01). The 2022 post "Pwede hulugan" ("you can pay in installments") got 89,000 views
  and 1,006 comments.
- **"Available pa po?"** (is it still in stock?) is the most common comment: 22% of fan comments.
- **Fans say they'll buy after they get a ticket.** 63 comments say so, almost all on SEVENTEEN posts from 2023.
  For example: "Babalikan ko ito kapag naka-secure na ako ng ticket" ("I'll come back once I get a ticket").
- **Lightstick setup is a real panic:** "half day akong nagkokokonek" ("I spent half a day trying to connect it").
  The most-liked question in our comment sample (283 likes) asks which app is the right one.
- **Trust questions** ("legit po?", "is it really from Korea?") come up on 10 of 29 videos we sampled.
- **"Will it arrive before the concert?"** matters more than the general shipping time.

**Demand for this right now looks real** (10: top results for 5 searches, mostly from outside the Philippines; a
small sample, so treat it as a direction):
- Army Bomb version 4 videos from small accounts (381 to 3,400 followers) reach 80,000 to 1.4 million views.
- "Official vs Fake Army Bomb" has 1 million views and 15,875 saves.
- A Filipino fan with 95 followers asking "any legit Army Bomb seller?" for their first BTS concert got 77,000 views.
- We found no Taglish version 4 tutorial in the results.

**The shop is already in position.** They run a BTS Army Bomb version 4 group order, and the box was due at the
Philippine port on Oct 16 (10). They sell mainly through X (18,662 followers, one sale post a day, about 700 to
1,100 views each), a Google Form and DMs. They also have a TikTok Shop and post on Facebook (02, 07, 10).

## 2. The strategy in one sentence

**Show up for every Philippine concert and lightstick launch with the help fans need in the six weeks before it,
and sell through the trust that help earns.**

That means being the lightstick and concert help desk for Filipino fans, in Taglish, run in seasons.

**How strong is the evidence?** (stated honestly, 09)
- **Own account:** 8 lightstick tutorials, median 21,000 views. The strongest were in 2022 to 2023 and January 2025.
  Of the last 4, the 2 hits were short step-by-step videos just before a Philippine concert; the 2 flops broke
  that format (01, 11).
- **Other shops:** one Indonesian lightstick shop with 5,939 followers gets 17 times its normal views from tutorials
  (5 videos). A US fan creator's KATSEYE tutorial got 453,600. We found no Philippine seller making them (03).
- **Search:** TikTok ranks Search mainly by how well a video matches what people type, with captions, on-screen
  text, subtitles and hashtags as the matching signals. Engagement counts too (05, 08). "How to connect [lightstick]"
  is what a fan types the night the lightstick arrives.
- **Not known yet:** whether tutorial views turn into orders. 23% of comments on the 225K tutorial were in Spanish,
  Indonesian or Korean, so many viewers can't buy here. We need the owner's order data from the week after that video.

**Seasons for the next 5 months** (06, 08). Priority goes by the *event*, not by which fandom the shop likes most:

| Season | Window | Why | Shop's position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BTS Bulacan** | Now to Mar 16, 2027 | 3 sold-out nights, Mar 13, 14 and 16. From June 12, 2026 on, only Army Bomb version 4 is wirelessly controlled at shows (official Weverse notice) | Version 4 stock arriving now. BTS has been the account's weakest fandom (1 of 21 posts doubled normal). The 2026 BTS tour news, ticketing tips and pre-order posts did about normal (0.6 to 1.25×). **No BTS lightstick tutorial has been tried yet**, and that's the bet |
| **ENHYPEN renewal** | October to November 2026 | New lightstick version (Renewal Edition) launches in October. Official: the old version 2 keeps working. Comeback targeted for November (date and format not set yet) | Core fandom: 41% of the shop's videos and both mega-hits |
| **Manila December shows** | Late Oct to Dec 12 | LE SSERAFIM, Dec 5 to 6; NCT 127, Dec 12 | Only if the shop stocks those lightsticks |
| **Early 2027 shows** | Dec to Feb | D.O. (EXO), Jan 23; NCT WISH, Feb 13; BIGBANG, Feb 20 | Only if stocked. The shop has sold EXO and YG lightsticks before |

SEVENTEEN, the shop's most reliable sales fandom, has no Philippine show in this window, and five members are
enlisting. Keep it light until there's news.

## 3. The season playbook (what to post in the six weeks before a show)

| Weeks before the show | What to post | Pain it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Ticket day (whenever it is) | "Ticket muna, lightstick later" ("ticket first, lightstick later"). Tell fans to secure tickets first, plus when the installment plan opens | Money, ticket-first |
| 6 to 4 weeks | **Ipon plan** ("savings plan"): the real installment ladder drawn as a calendar that finishes before the show | Money (#1 pain) |
| 6 to 2 weeks | **Lightstick Clinic:** connect to the app, "will my old version work?", real vs fake, battery and care | Setup panic, trust |
| 3 to 2 weeks | **"Aabot ba?"** ("will it arrive in time?"): last order date by city, same-day delivery or Cubao pickup before the show | Timing |
| Show week | Concert-day lightstick checklist; pairing-booth info when the official notice comes out; the owner at the show if they go | Panic at the venue |
| After | Thanks and recap; leftover stock wall | Proof |

**Between seasons**, post less: proof videos and answers to real fan questions.

## 4. What to post each week

**Target: 3 videos a week, 4 as a stretch** (09: 4 is too much for a 1 to 2 person shop with everything else
this plan adds). Posting more mostly buys more chances at a breakout. The typical video stays about the same (05, 08).

| Kind | How often | What | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lightstick Clinic** | 1 to 2 a week in season | One lightstick and one problem per video. Hands only, filmed from above, 30 to 60 seconds | Own tutorials before concerts: 30K to 225K (01). Search demand (10) | Strongest bet, when timed and in the short step format |
| **Money and installments** | 2 a month | "Ipon plan", how the installment works, "what happens if I'm late?" | #1 pain (04); installment posts get 8× more comments per view; 89K in 2022 (01) | Proven in 2022; test now |
| **Proof** | 1 a week | Weekly stock wall of what's really on hand; "box from Korea" episodes | Two stock-wall hits, both inside concert windows (01, 02). "100% legit" claim posts score only 0.2 to 0.6× (03) | Promising (n=2); the box series is a test |
| **News + keyword** | Only with real news | Owner on camera: the news first, then "comment [one word] to reserve" | News-first got 33 comments vs 2 for price-first, same presenter (02). That post may have been paid (owner question 4) | Test |
| **Answer a fan** | 2 a month | A real fan question, re-typed on screen without their name (privacy), answered clearly | A 2023 installment reply got 42,500 (in a SEVENTEEN window); a 2025 one got 1,342 (01) | Test |

**Real fan moments** (the owner at a concert or cinema event) are welcome, but we don't plan around them. The
226K "Fate Tour" video was a Weverse video call, and the concert content the shop has posted since 2024 is its weakest
type (0.6× normal, 01).

**Stop doing on TikTok:**
- **Price-list slideshows and WTS text walls as standalone posts.** In 7 of 11 accounts, slideshows did worse than the
  same shop's videos (03). Keep the full list on X, in the caption and in the pinned comment.
- **Reposting the same offer week after week.** The BABYMONSTER lightstick went 1,186 → 629 → 598 → 430 → 446 views
  over five posts (02), while the account as a whole was also slipping.
- **Memes and generic "fan account" posts.** None of the 10 in the last 12 months doubled normal; median 0.8× (01).
- **Captions that read like AI.** Some recent ones contain "Suggested title and hashtags" or markdown stars (01).
- ~~The Heeseung video-call clip.~~ Answered 2026-10-14: the call is hers, so it stays pinned. She has calls with
  every member, and her own call clips are her second-best format by results (55,900 and 226K views; see
  `OWNER-ANSWERS.md`). Worth testing as real fan moments.

## 5. Rules for every video (short version; full cards in `PLAYBOOK.md`)

1. **The real item in the first frame. A visible payoff in the first 2 to 4 seconds**, the faster the better (the
   225K tutorial lit up at 1.3 s, the 30K one at 2.5 s, 02).
2. **Don't open with the full price.** If you lead with money, lead with the small number and show the total on the
   same line: "₱300 DP · total ₱2,750". We'll A/B test this "small number first" hook against "help first" (02 Card E).
3. **Say the search phrase out loud, write it on screen, and turn on subtitles.** TikTok matches Search on captions,
   on-screen text, subtitles and hashtags (08). Use 3 to 5 exact hashtags with the model name; skip #fyp.
4. **Sound: the owner's voice, or TikTok's Commercial Music Library.** TikTok's rule is that businesses use the
   Commercial Music Library for all their posts, not just ads (08). Trending K-pop songs are a risk on every post.
   TikTok's built-in text reader is fine for step lists. Never an AI voice pretending to be a person.
5. **"Your brand" disclosure on** any post that sells, including a Clinic video with a selling pinned comment (05).
6. **Date or version claims are scheduled at most 48 hours ahead,** or re-checked the morning they post. Scheduled
   TikTok posts can't be edited (08).
7. **No fake scarcity.** "Last 2 pieces" only if the stock count says so.

## 7. Fix the sales path (small leaks)

| Leak | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Available pa po?" asked 220 times | Weekly stock wall with real counts + a pinned "on hand this week" comment |
| Price differs between TikTok Shop and the form ("what's the real price?") | One all-in price everywhere, or explain the TikTok Shop fee once and pin it |

| Fear of failing an installment | Explain the real late-payment rule in one video |

| Most on-hand items not linked to TikTok Shop | Tag them. Peers tag 22 to 38% of videos; the shop tagged 2% of its latest 50. Weak signal, cheap to try (03) |

## 9. Keeping it human (no AI slop)

| Machine does | Human does |
|---|---|
| Watches news, ticket sites and Weverse notices | Decides which GOs to open and at what price |
| Pulls stats and comments every week | Films with real hands, real items and a real voice |
| Shows which videos beat normal | Picks the final caption voice and sound |
| Drafts a weekly video list, each item with a source | Approves or kills each idea |
| Drafts captions (prices filled from the order sheet once Phase B exists) | Talks to customers; handles complaints and refunds |
| Builds the order sheet, lookup page and reminders | Confirms every payment |

**The slop gate** blocks a draft before the owner sees it when any of these apply (same 6 rules as `AUTOMATION.md`):
1. No source.
2. A number that isn't in the source or the order sheet. Converted times and computed totals are allowed if they
   name what they came from.
3. Scarcity the stock count doesn't show.
4. A banned phrase (list in `PLAYBOOK.md`).
5. AI faces, AI voices, stock renders as the main shot, or someone else's footage.
6. The same offer posted in the last 14 days.

Every factual sentence in a brief (dates, versions, "only version 4 syncs", app names) must quote the source line it
rests on, and a human ticks it.

## 10. What gets automated, in order

Details are in `AUTOMATION.md`. All numbers come from research 07's line items and are **estimates**. They assume
2 to 4 open GOs, about 50 active orders and 40 to 80 DMs a week, which the owner hasn't confirmed yet.

| Phase | What | Andrey's hours | Owner's hours | Cost/month | Time saved/week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Content brain** | News and calendar watcher, weekly stats and comment report, peer outliers, weekly video brief with shot lists and caption drafts, banned-phrase checker | 19 to 27 | 5 min/week (stock list + question tally) | $6 to 9 | ~1.8 to 2 h (mostly news scrolling; caption drafts speed up once the Phase B order sheet exists) |
| **B · Order desk** | One order sheet, "Check my order" page, 9 AM owner summary, saved Taglish replies, free scheduler | ~21 | 4 to 5 | $0 | ~1.5 to 2.7 h |
| **C · Optional, one piece at a time** | Payment-screenshot pre-check, reminder drafts, viral-spike comment triage, payment links, DM bot test, restock watcher | 40 to 50 if all built | varies | $30 to 46 + payment fees (1.34 to 2.23% per payment) | ~2.5 to 4.5 h if all built |

**Why Phase A first:** it's the only phase that doesn't wait on the owner's answers, and it drives growth, which is
the main goal. Phase B saves a similar amount of the owner's time and fixes the sales leaks, so we start it as soon
as owner questions 7 to 9 are answered. The two can run side by side.

**Not automated, on purpose:** our own TikTok posting app (TikTok rejects it), bots logged into the shop's accounts,
auto-likes or comments, payments marked "paid" without the owner, and anything a customer reads that the owner
hasn't approved (full list in `AUTOMATION.md`).

## 11. How we'll know it works

**Starting point** (01; each with its time window):
- Median views: 608 per video in 2026
- Saves: 0.09% of views (last 12 months)
- Posts with zero comments: 53% (last 12 months)
- Volume: 7 posts in the last 90 days
- Tutorials: 1 in the last 12 months
- Orders per week: unknown. We'll set it in week 1 from the owner's records.

| Measure | Target by mid-January 2027 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | 3 a week, about 36 in 12 weeks, at least 12 of them Clinic | Volume buys chances |
| Clinic videos vs normal | Median at least 2× the account's normal | The realistic floor |
| Clinic saves | Median 0.35% of views or more (stretch: 0.5%) | 0.35% is the shop's own tutorial median (01) |
| Breakouts | 3 videos above 10,000 views in 12 weeks | Last 12 months: 1 clean one (two looked paid, one was reused footage) |
| Posts with zero comments | Under 40% | From 53% |
| X views per sale post | Tracked weekly (now about 700 to 1,100, 10) | The order desk's reach |
| Orders from TikTok and X | Tracked weekly: a "where did you see us?" question on the form and a saved DM reply | Views aren't the goal; orders are |
| Search share on Clinic videos | Measured in TikTok Studio | Tells us whether the search bet is right |

**Kill rule:** if the first 4 Clinic videos all land near normal views, cut Clinic to one every two weeks until the
next season and move the effort to proof and money videos.

**Check points:**
- **Day 30:** are we posting on schedule, and how are the Clinic save rates?
- **Day 60:** keep a format only if at least 4 videos of it have a median above 1.5× normal. Drop the rest.
- **Day 90:** go all in for the BTS season, or change course.

**Honest limits:**
- Traffic source is now known account-wide (Search 77.1%, For You 18.7%; `OWNER-ANSWERS.md`). Watch time and
  per-video search share are still missing.
- Most format evidence rests on small samples, and some hits may have been paid.
- Small accounts are being squeezed across TikTok, so good content may still give modest numbers.
- Tutorials reach fans abroad who can't buy here.
- Several facts rest on one source (see 06 and 08).
