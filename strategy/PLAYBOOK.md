# Unnie Mart playbook: how to film each video

Version 2 · 2026-10-14. For the person filming and editing. Short on purpose.
Evidence: `research/02-video-breakdown.md` (VB), `research/03-competitors.md` (CO), `research/04-audience.md` (AU),
`research/05-algorithm-strategy.md` (AL), `research/08-fact-check.md` (FC). Words like GO, DP, DOP, ETA, self mode,
the "Your brand" switch and the Commercial Music Library are explained at the top of `STRATEGY.md`.

**All Taglish lines in this file are placeholders.** The owner rewrites them in their own words.

---

## Rules for every video

1. **First frame = the real thing.** The lightstick, the box wall, the moment. Not a face on a plain wall, a stock
   photo or a screenshot of a Facebook post. (VB: 4 of the 6 hits open on the real item; the other 2 open on the idol)
   Exceptions: News videos (Card D) open on the official announcement for one second; Answer-a-fan videos (Card E)
   can open on you holding the thing you're explaining.
2. **A visible payoff in the first 2 to 4 seconds, the faster the better.** The light turns on, the box opens, the
   camera pans. (VB: 1.3 s on the 225K video, 2.5 s on the 30K one)
3. **Don't open with the full price.** If you lead with money, lead with the small number and put the total in the
   same line: "₱300 DP · total ₱2,750". (AU: a ₱100 DP shown alone was misread as a fake price. We're testing
   "small number first" against "help first".)
4. **Say the search phrase out loud, put it on screen, and turn on subtitles.** TikTok matches search on captions,
   on-screen text, subtitles and hashtags (FC). Then add 3 to 5 exact hashtags with the model name (#armybombv4,
   #engenebong). No #fyp, #foryou or #viral (AL).
5. **Text top-left or centre, never over the product.** TikTok's buttons sit bottom-right. (Good practice. One hit
   had its steps bottom-right and still worked, so this is a rule of thumb.)
6. **No logo end card.** End on the payoff. (A guess until we see retention data.)
7. **Sound: your own voice, or TikTok's Commercial Music Library.** TikTok says businesses must use it for all their
   posts, organic ones too (FC). Trending K-pop songs are a risk on every post. TikTok's built-in text reader is OK
   for step lists. Never an AI voice pretending to be a person.
8. **"Your brand" disclosure on** for any post that sells, including a Clinic with a selling pinned comment (AL).
9. **Date or version claims: schedule at most 48 hours ahead,** or re-check the morning of posting. Scheduled posts
   can't be edited (FC).
10. **Save a clean copy** (exported from CapCut or the camera roll, not downloaded from TikTok) for Facebook Reels and X (AL).
11. **Post from a Philippine phone when you can.** TikTok uses the posting region as one input; we'll test it.

## Card A · Lightstick Clinic (1 to 2 a week, in season)

**Evidence:** the shop's short step-by-step tutorials posted 4 days to 5 weeks before a Philippine concert got 30K
to 225K views. The two recent flops broke the format (an 82-second video with a long intro; a "watch it on YouTube"
pointer), and one also had no concert coming (01, 11). Version 4 tutorials by small accounts get 80K to 165K (10,
mostly outside the Philippines).

- **Topics:** connect to the app · "will my old version work at [show]?" · real vs fake · battery in and out ·
  "concert mode says no concert" (normal at home) · "wrong PIN" · version 1 vs version 2 · care between concerts
  (don't leave batteries inside) · where the pairing booths are (when the official notice is out).
- **Before filming:** a **demo unit** (not a buyer's sealed item) and the **official manual**. Steps differ by group
  and version (AU 3.5). Search TikTok for the topic and note what's already there.
- **Shot:** from above, hands only, one take. Unlit lightstick + phone with the app open, both in frame.
- **0 to 2 s:** the title on screen ("How to connect Army Bomb VER.4 to the app") and the first step starts.
- **By 2 to 4 s:** the light turns on.
- **Steps:** 3 to 4 numbered steps on screen, each 2 to 4 seconds. Connected by about 12 s.
- **Payoff:** 15 to 25 s of colours changing every 2 to 3 seconds.
- **Length:** 30 to 60 s; longer only if the steps need it.
- **Voice:** a short Taglish voice-over saying the steps, or text only with soft music. Subtitles on.
- **Caption (placeholder):** "How to connect Army Bomb VER.4 to the app 💡 Para po sa mga nahirapan mag-connect,
  step by step." + #armybombv4 #bts #lightstick #kpopph
- **Selling:** none in the video. Pinned comment: "May on hand po kami ng [model] · ₱300 DP · total ₱X · details sa bio."
- **Series look:** the same cover every episode ("Lightstick Clinic #4 · Army Bomb VER.4").

## Card B · Proof (1 a week)

**Evidence:** stock walls did 71.6K (Oct 2023) and 11.9K (Nov 2025), both inside SEVENTEEN tour windows. A 2026
arrival clip with no window got 416 (01, 02). "100% legit" claim posts score 0.2 to 0.6× (CO).

**B1 · Stock wall (6 to 12 s)**
- One wide, sharp, well-lit shot of many of the same boxes. A slow pan, or a still shot.
- 3 to 5 words on screen: "On hand na po · pwede hulugan".
- Optional last second: open one box and switch it on.
- Don't use blurry close-ups, items still in plastic bags, or a date as the only text (VB L2: 416 views).

**B2 · "Box from Korea" episodes (series)**
- Official order screen (personal data blurred) → box arrives → opening → sorting → packing with names covered →
  courier hand-off. Short text labels, little talking.
- 45 to 75 s. End: "Ep. 2 next box."
- Evidence is thin: one Philippine GOM's 2021 "day in the life" did 151× (CO, n=1). Treat it as a test.

**B3 · Packing orders (milestones only)**
- A big batch or a milestone only ("first Army Bomb batch"). Pack-with-me is the most overused format in the niche
  and rarely beats normal (CO).

## Card C · Money and installments (2 a month)

**Evidence:** money is pain #1 (AU). Installment posts get 8× more comments per view (01). "Pwede hulugan" got 89K
in 2022 (01). Recent results are unknown, so this is a test.

- **Ipon plan:** draw the real installment ladder on paper or a planner as a calendar that ends before the show.
  "₱300 DP ngayon → ₱X by [date] → on hand before [show]."
- **"What happens if I'm late?":** the shop's real rule, said kindly and clearly.
- **"Ticket muna, lightstick later":** on ticket day, tell fans to get the ticket first and when installments open.
- **A/B test:** half the money videos open with the small number ("₱300 DP lang…") over the real stock; half open
  with help first. Compare after 6 videos.

## Card D · News + keyword (only with real news; a test)

**Evidence:** news-first got 10,300 views and 33 comments; price-first got 629 and 2, same presenter and wall (VB M1
vs L1). The views may have been paid (owner question 4); the comment gap is the stronger signal.

- **0 to 2 s:** the official announcement visual for 1 second, then you. Vary the opener each time. Examples (placeholders):
  - "Engenes, may balita po!"
  - "ARMY, open na ang pre-order!"

  For a pre-order, don't say "it's here".
- **What dropped:** official photos, 5 to 15 s.
- **Dates in Philippine time,** as stickers.
- **The service:** "Sa mga hindi kayang mag-order sa Weverse on their own, kami na po bahala."
- **Ask:** "Comment '[ONE WORD]' para sa schedule" (for example RENEWAL). Reply to each one.
- **Caption:** the all-in price ladder in plain words: "₱300 DP · full payment ₱2,650 · by [date] ₱2,750 · kasama na
  po shipping at packing · ETA [weeks]". Source link in a comment.
- **Length:** 40 to 60 s.

## Card E · Answer a fan (2 a month)

**Evidence:** a 2023 installment reply got 42,500 (in a SEVENTEEN window); a 2025 one got 1,342 (01).

- **Privacy first:** don't use TikTok's reply-with-video on an old comment. It shows the person's name and photo.
  Instead, re-type the question as on-screen text: "Tanong ng isang buyer: …". Or reply to a recent comment after
  asking the commenter.
- Topics:
  - "What happens if I'm late with a payment?"
  - "Aabot ba sa concert?" with cut-off dates by city
  - How to order in 20 seconds (TikTok Shop / form / X)
  - What DP, PAYO and DOP mean, for new fans
  - Why prices differ between shops
  - Gift guide for parents: "which version should I buy for my daughter?" (Facebook-first)
- Face on camera is fine. Show the thing you're explaining.
- 45 to 120 s. Be specific: real dates, real amounts, your real rules.

## Bonus · Real fan moments (not planned around)

- Only your own footage: concert, cinema cheering party, fan event, pop-up.
- Your face, voice and story should be most of the video. Use short performance cuts with the performance audio
  muted or turned down under your voice, never full songs (AL, and the music rule above).

## Banned (the slop list)

- **Phrases:**
  - "this might change your life", "hear me out", "stay till the end", "game changer", "you won't believe",
    "must-have", "Suggested title and hashtags", markdown stars (\*\*like this\*\*)
  - "don't miss out" without a real date
  - "LAST 2 PCS" without a real count
  - "100% legit" as a claim (show proof instead)
- **Visuals:** AI faces, AI idols, AI product photos, stock renders as the main shot, other people's fancams or watermarks.
- **Audio:** AI voices that imitate a person; trending K-pop singles on shop posts.
- **Habits:** reposting the same offer within 14 days; memes unrelated to K-pop or the shop; fear-selling ("your old
  one is useless").
