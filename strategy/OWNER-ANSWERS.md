1. **The search bet is confirmed.** About 3 in 4 views come from people searching, not from the For You feed.
   Titles, on-screen text, subtitles and hashtags with the exact model name matter more than trends.
2. **Tutorials keep earning for years.** Three of the top 10 are tutorials posted 1 to 4 years ago (92,000, 8,719
   and 3,306 views this year alone). A Clinic video is a long-term asset, not a one-week post.
3. **About a third of viewers can't buy** (34.5% outside the Philippines, Indonesia the biggest). Normal for
   tutorials. Q13 (does she ship abroad?) is worth asking in round 2.
4. **Few viewers visit the profile** (2,900 profile views from 309,700 views, under 1%). The sales path from a
   video needs to be in the video itself: pinned comment, Shop tag, "details in bio".
5. **Retention and per-video search share are still missing.** These screenshots are account-wide. When she has a
   minute: the same "Traffic source" box on the engenebong V2 tutorial and the Heeseung clip.
