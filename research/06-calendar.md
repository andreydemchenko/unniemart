# 06 · K-pop events calendar: mid-Oct 2026 to June 2027

Compiled 2026-10-14 for Unnie Mart (TikTok @unniemart_). Machine-readable copy: `data/calendar.csv` (144 rows, same data).

**How to read it**
- **Confidence:** `high` = official notice (Weverse, label, promoter) or a named outlet quoting the agency/promoter. `medium` = one secondary source or an aggregator (kpopofficial, Soompi masterlist, Billboard PH guide), not cross-checked. `expected` = not announced; based on last year's pattern, with the pattern's source. `rumor` = unconfirmed or conflicting.
- **Dates:** `YYYY-MM` means only the month is known. `TBA` means no date at all. Times are KST unless marked PHT (PHT = KST minus 1 hour).
- Everything is as of 2026-10-14. Dates move. Re-check anything `medium` before you film around it.

## 2. The 10 biggest moments for this shop

Ranked by the likely demand for lightsticks and merch, and by fit with what has already worked for the account. Proof points from the account come from `data/raw/tiktok-popular-2026-10-14.json`: "Connecting engenebong V2 to the app" has 225K views, the Fate Tour PH post 226K, Caratbong posts 42–72K, and "ENHYPEN SG 2023 for installment" 43K.

Album plus lightstick installment bundles fit here. *(high on month)*

JYP has posted TWICE ("Spotless") and Stray Kids ("Paper Tale") teasers, but I did not verify their pre-order dates. 5. **Christmas, with 13th-month pay due by Dec 24.** This is when gift budgets land in PH ([DOLE BWC](https://bwc.dole.gov.ph/dole-bwc-urges-employers-to-release-13th-month-pay-not-later-than-december-24/)). 6. **HYBE CINE FEST, Oct 24–31, and the BTS live viewings in PH cinemas on Oct 25 and Oct 31.** The festival includes a 3D "cheering party" where fans wave lightsticks in the cinema. That makes a quick, timely "bring your lightstick, check battery and app" post, and the first PH group Armybomb moment before Bulacan ([Billboard PH](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/hybe-cine-fest-2026-bts-concert-films-philippines/)). *(high)*
7. **LE SSERAFIM PUREFLOW in Manila, Dec 5–6 at SM MOA Arena.** Two arena nights, with ticketing already done. Concert-prep lightstick content fits the two weeks before. *(high)*
8. **NCT in Manila: NCT 127 on Dec 12 at MOA Arena, with tickets Oct 17 (presale) and Oct 18 (general); NCT WISH on Feb 13, 2027 at Araneta, tickets TBA.** That gives two NCT concerts and three ticketing moments in four months. Check which NCT lightstick version each show syncs with before making claims. *(high)*
9. **The early-2027 Manila run-up before BTS: D.O. on Jan 23 (Araneta), BIGBANG on Feb 20 (SMDC Festival Grounds) and Young K on Feb 20 (New Frontier).** These are older fandoms with their own lightsticks, and the shop has sold EXO and YG lightsticks before. D.O. ticketing is TBA, which will be a content moment when it is announced. *(high)*
10. **TXT: PERFECT STORM on Nov 16 and the STEAL THE WIND tour.** No Manila date yet. The announced SEA stops are KL on Mar 13 and Jakarta on Mar 20, and "further stops" are promised ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/08/tomorrow-x-together-2026-2027-steal-the-wind-world-tour-dates/)). Album GO now; if Manila gets added, it jumps to the top 5. *(high)*

Honorable mentions:
