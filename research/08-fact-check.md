## What the plan must change

1. **Scheduler window: 10 days, not 30.** `research/05` recommendation 6 says "up to 30 days ahead". Change it to
   15 min to 10 days, and note that posts can't be edited after scheduling. `AUTOMATION.md` already says 10 days,
   so keep that. Batch-film weekly, not monthly.
2. **ENHYPEN November = a target month, not a date.** In `STRATEGY.md` §1 and the November row: write "comeback
   targeted for November (date and format TBA; may be a special or expanded album)". Don't open album GOs or bundle
   prices until BELIFT posts the schedule.
3. **Search wording.** Don't present "voiceover" as something TikTok officially says. Correct version: TikTok ranks
   Search on relevance from captions, on-screen text and hashtags, and tells creators to use the keyword "in your
   video, subtitles, and captions". Engagement still counts. The practical rule doesn't change: say the keyword out
   loud **and** turn on auto-captions or subtitles so the spoken words become text.
4. **Army Bomb explainer (idea #1).** The official notice only says wireless control is VER.4-only from June 12.
   "Old ones still light up" is not in the notice. Show it on camera as the shop's own demo instead of citing
   "official". In-app pairing booth locations are official ("venue map and pairing booth locations within the
   Light Stick app").
5. **Heeseung is now EVAN.** Use "EVAN (formerly Heeseung)" in anything public-facing (calendar birthday entry,
   OWNER-QUESTIONS #5 context). He is still a BELIFT artist, which is relevant if the shop sells his solo album.
6. **Commercial music applies to the whole shop.** TikTok's rule covers businesses' organic content, not just
   "Business accounts" or "sales posts". `PLAYBOOK.md` rule 8 should say: any shop post uses the CML or original
   voice. Trending K-pop singles are a risk on every post, Clinic tutorials included.
7. **GCash for Business prerequisites.** Change `OWNER-QUESTIONS.md` #7 to ask about DTI **and** BIR (2303)
   registration, plus a GCash account verified for 12+ months. Without these, the payment-link phase (07) is blocked.
8. **Facebook vs TikTok reach.** If the number is quoted, compare adults with adults: Facebook 95.6M (18+) vs TikTok
   64.0M (18+), both as ad reach, not users. The conclusion, that Facebook reaches more Filipinos, still stands.
9. **13th-month framing.** Use it for working fans and parents ("gift for your fan" guide), not students. Some
   employers pay half mid-year and many pay in early December, so gift posts from early December are right.
10. **No change needed** for claims 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11 and 13. Recheck the ENHYPEN renewal sale notice on Weverse
    daily for the rest of October.
