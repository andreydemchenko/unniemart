  From the Busan concert on June 12, 2026 onward") · [GMA, third Bulacan date](https://www.gmanetwork.com/entertainment/showbiznews/news/134565/bts-adds-third-bulacan-concert-for-arirang-tour/story)

  1. 0 s: an older Army Bomb and a VER.4 side by side, both off. Text: "May VER.3 ka? Para po sa Bulacan."

     in self mode at home?).

  vs VER.4)" #armybomb #armybombv4 #btsbulacan #bts #kpopph
