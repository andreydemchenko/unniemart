# When to post your member call clips

Written so Andrey can forward it to her as is. Birthdays come from kprofiles
(https://kprofiles.com/enhypen-members-profile/), events from `data/calendar.csv`.

---

**The simple rule: post each member's call on his birthday.**
On a birthday, lots of fans search for that member's name, and most of your views come from search. So a call
clip posted that day has the best chance of being found.

| Date | Member | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 15 (tomorrow) | Heeseung (EVAN) | Optional, since you're travelling. Your Heeseung call is already your 2nd most-watched video. |
| Oct 20 to 22 | Any member | ENHYPEN's concert film plays in PH cinemas during HYBE CINE FEST (Oct 24 to 31). Post before it starts. |
| Nov (date not announced) | Any member | Comeback week. Wait for the official date. |
| Nov 15 | Jake | Birthday |
| Nov 30 | All members (one each, or a short mix) | ENHYPEN's 6th debut anniversary |
| Dec 8 | Sunghoon | Birthday |
| Dec 9 | Ni-ki | Birthday |
| Feb 9 | Jungwon | Birthday |
| Apr 20 | Jay | Birthday |
| Jun 24 | Sunoo | Birthday |

**Three small tips**
1. Post on the birthday itself, Philippine date.
2. In the caption, write the member's name the way fans search it (for example "Jake"), plus "birthday".
3. Pin a comment that points to whatever you're selling that week. The clip brings people in, and the pinned
   comment is how they order.

At most one call clip a week (Sunghoon and Ni-ki are the exception, a day apart), so your page still looks like a
shop.
