# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **The owner** of Unnie Mart, a one-person K-pop group-order (GO) merch shop in the Philippines. She runs it from her
  phone (Samsung S23 Ultra), often between DM replies, in daylight or on the move. She films and posts the shop's
  TikToks herself. Her masterlist (a Google Sheet with 100+ GO tabs) is how she has always tracked orders, and it
  stays the source of truth.
- **Andrey**, who built the automations and plans the content with her. Uses a MacBook, mostly in the evening:
  reviews the weekly video ideas, checks the numbers, keeps tasks moving.
- Later maybe her helper (replies to DMs, records payments). Not now.

The job: see who owes what and when, record a payment the moment a GCash or Maya screenshot arrives, send a kind
reminder, know what is still in stock, and know which video to film next and why.

## Product Purpose

One private place to run the shop: orders and balances (live, two-way with her Google Sheet), stock, the weekly
content plan with its sources, to-dos, how the videos are doing, and the plan behind it on one screen. Success: she opens it instead of scrolling the sheet, payments get recorded the same day, and
the videos in the plan actually get filmed.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, clear, trustworthy. It should feel like a good banking app (GCash, KakaoBank, Toss, Monzo): money is
easy to read, buttons are big, nothing is hidden behind jargon. Warm without being cute. Short plain English; the
shop's own Taglish only where a message goes to a buyer.

## Anti-references

- The previous order desk: purple-to-pink gradients, blurred colour blobs, frosted glass, gradient avatars,
  count-up hero numbers on dark violet. Everything that reads as "AI made this".
- K-pop pastel clichés (pink and lavender everything, sparkles, emoji in headings).
- SaaS dashboard templates: identical stat cards, icon-heading-text grids, labels explaining labels.
- Spreadsheet-in-a-browser: dense grey tables with every column she already has in Google Sheets.

## Design Principles

1. **Money first, in plain words.** "₱2,650 · due Friday" beats a table row. Every amount has a person and a date.
2. **One tap for the common thing.** Paid, remind, slot taken, done: one tap and a confirm, from the list itself.
3. **Her sheet stays the truth.** The site never hides a change: what it writes is visible in the sheet and its log,
   and what she types in the sheet shows up here within seconds.
4. **Show the source.** Ideas carry the fan quote or link they came from. Numbers say when they were measured.
5. **Calm until it matters.** Colour and motion mark state (late, paid, new), never decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Tap targets at least 44px. Works one-handed on a large phone. Honors reduced motion and the
system light/dark setting. Status is never shown by colour alone (always a word or icon too).
